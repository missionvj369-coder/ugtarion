# Universal Guard Trust - Brand Guidelines

## 🎯 Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** Universal Guard Trust (UGT)
**Tagline:** "Your Universal Identity, Your Universal Trust"
**Mission:** To provide a secure, unified identity system that transcends borders and boundaries.
**Vision:** A world where every individual has a verifiable, universal identity that ensures trust and security.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity

### Logo
The UGT logo represents protection, unity, and global reach through a shield motif combined with a globe element.

**Primary Logo Usage:**
- Use the full logo with tagline for marketing materials
- Use the icon-only version for app icons and favicons
- Minimum clear space: 1x the height of the logo on all sides

## 🖼️ Imagery & Photography

### Style Guidelines
- **Professional & Trustworthy:** Use clean, professional imagery
- **Diverse & Inclusive:** Feature people of all ages, ethnicities, and backgrounds
- **Global Perspective:** Show diverse locations and settings
- **Technology-Forward:** Include modern devices, secure environments

### Image Categories
1. **Hero Images:** Global connectivity, trust, security
2. **Team Photos:** Authentic, professional headshots
3. **Lifestyle:** Real people using the platform
4. **Icons:** Simple, recognizable, consistent stroke width

## 🔤 Logo Usage Rules

### DO ✅
- Use the official logo files provided
- Maintain clear space around the logo
- Use approved color variations only
- Scale proportionally

### DON'T ❌
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Change logo colors outside approved palette
- Add effects (shadows, gradients) to the logo
- Use low-resolution versions
- Place logo on busy backgrounds without contrast

## 📱 Application in Digital

### Website
- Primary Blue (#1E40AF) for primary CTAs
- Trust Teal (#0D9488) for secondary actions
- Deep Navy (#0F172A) for text
- Light Gray (#F8FAFC) for section backgrounds

### Mobile App
- Follow same color palette
- Larger touch targets (minimum 44x44px)
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Consistent iconography

### Email
- Header: Primary Blue background
- Body: White background, Deep Navy text
- CTAs: Trust Teal buttons
- Footer: Light Gray background

---

## 🖨️ Print Applications

### Business Cards
- Front: Logo, Name, Title
- Back: Contact information, QR code
- Standard size: 3.5" x 2"

### Letterhead
- Logo top left
- Contact info footer
- Clean, professional layout

### Presentations
- Title slides: Deep Navy background
- Content slides: White background
- Consistent header/footer

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Inconsistent Colors:** Always use exact hex codes
2. **Wrong Fonts:** Never substitute fonts
3. **Low Quality Images:** Always use high-resolution assets
4. **Unclear Hierarchy:** Follow the typography scale
5. **Cluttered Design:** Maintain adequate white space
6. **Inconsistent Spacing:** Use the spacing system
